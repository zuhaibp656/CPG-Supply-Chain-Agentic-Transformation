Workforce & workflow map

Agentic Transformation Across Workforce & Workflows

Mapping 60 Consumer Packaged Goods (CPG) Supply Chain roles and 720 operational workflows to show which specific value levers each agent moves — and where ERP systems and human sign-offs stay in charge.

Hover any square to inspect the workflow and its exact 4-lever rupee attribution here. Click any square to pin it while you present.

Each row is one CPG role ordered left to right: AI Agent prepares (Critical → Friction → Routine), then Incumbent ERP/WMS/TMS runs it, then Human-only Sign-Off holds final authority.

Every agent moves only the 2 or 3 levers it genuinely impacts (Productivity, Flow & Capital, Risk & Leakage, or Revenue Recovery); untouched levers stay explicitly marked — so nothing is double-counted or inflated.

Google Cloud. Every agent stops at a human decision — none can commit an unapproved purchase order, release quarantined QA stock, or alter statutory Maximum Retail Price (MRP) records. Row values sum non-double-counted annual impact across the four CPG levers in Indian Crores (≥ ₹1 Cr) and Lakhs (< ₹1 Cr).
